How it works
REPLAY STUDIO is inspired by Choicer Voicer: you replace one character's voice with yours while everyone else keeps their original performance. The built-in library uses public-domain films (Night of the Living Dead 1968, Plan 9 from Outer Space 1959, The Little Shop of Horrors 1960) via archive.org. For anything modern, use Create a Scene with a video you own.
Optional: AI transcription for uploads
Paste a free Google AI Studio key to auto-transcribe uploaded scenes. It stays on this device. The rest of the app never needs a key.
